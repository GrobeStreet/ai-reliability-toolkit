# Seller inspection and launch decision

Prepared November 16, 2026. Operational reading of Agensi's published pages;
not legal advice or confirmation that a creator account has been approved.

## Recommended commercial setup

Recommend **$19 one-time** for version 0.3.0. This is a price experiment,
not evidence of willingness to pay. The original public toolkit remains MIT.
The proposition is a focused, executable workflow delivered conveniently to
an agent, not exclusive access to an unpublished scientific method.

Observed asking prices, not observed sales: Experiment Replication Pack $5;
Dataset Quality Audit $5; Quant Research Toolkit $19; SOC2 & HIPAA Compliance
Code Auditor $29. These differ in scope. They establish an asking-price range,
not a market-clearing price. Our narrower benchmark focus and runnable paired
checks justify testing above a general checklist, while $19 reduces the first
purchase hurdle relative to the previously floated $29. Measure demand before
expanding or claiming traction. No revenue forecast is made.

At $19, a 70% share is **$13.30 per undiscounted, non-refunded sale**, excluding
buyer taxes and the creator's own income taxes. This is unit arithmetic, not
an earnings projection. Refunds and chargebacks can reverse the share.

## Published terms inspected

- Creators keep 70% of net sale price; the 30% platform fee includes processing.
- One-time purchases from $5 allow card payment. Paid skills require enabled
  payouts; US creators can use Stripe Connect. Existing Stripe access here
  does not establish an Agensi Stripe Connect relationship.
- Card earnings transfer to Stripe at sale; balance-funded earnings transfer
  after pending amounts reach $3.50. Bank availability follows Stripe's payout
  schedule, not a promised immediate deposit.
- Agensi is merchant of record, handles buyer sales taxes, and issues self-billed
  invoices for the creator share. Creator legal/tax details must be accurate.
- Paid listings have the platform's 30-day guarantee against the listing's
  promises. No separate refund policy should be written into our listing.
- No outside checkout links or off-platform paid upsells are permitted in the
  listing, examples, or package. Links to source/docs are allowed.
- Creator ownership is retained; Agensi receives a nonexclusive worldwide
  delivery/display licence. Buyers' platform access is restricted by Agensi's
  licence. Because this package is also public MIT source, do not claim that
  those restrictions revoke existing source permissions or make copying
  impossible. Confirm the source disclosure is accepted in the actual form.
- Buyers receive skills through their connected agents. Bundled scripts are
  delivered as text and executed unchanged from temporary files. The scripts
  therefore work independently of neighboring package files.
- One-time buyers receive approved updates if offered; no perpetual support,
  custom consulting, unlimited maintenance or model execution is promised.
- Skills that have buyers cannot simply be deleted in the dashboard; ongoing
  buyer access matters when choosing what to publish.
- The terms permit revenue-share changes with 30 days' notice. Listing review
  and platform availability are not guaranteed.

The public terms still describe 72-hour USDC settlement while the payouts docs
describe 14 days for newer sales. That discrepancy is unresolved. Recommend
Stripe Connect for this US-based launch; do not use a crypto payout promise.

## Actual access state and remaining steps

The submission URL redirects to sign-in. The available browser profile has no
recorded Agensi sign-in. No Agensi creator connector is exposed. Account,
Creator Mode, payout eligibility, name availability, support-file delivery and
an actual marketplace run remain unverified. No credentials were requested or
retrieved, no account or payout configuration was changed, and no email sent.

The user must preview the completed listing before submission. After approval,
sign in with bobby@trainingties.com, enable Creator Mode if needed, connect
Stripe payouts with accurate details, upload the selected ZIP, populate the
listing fields and verify the host's preview. Do not create a duplicate listing
if an existing draft or listing is found. Do not enable paid Agensi tools: this
package needs no hosted tool, API or model service to run its bundled demos.
If the form or scan requires changes, retain the exact reason and fix it.
Submit only the user-approved content and price. Review is not publication;
verify the resulting listing and, when available, its delivered resources.

## Success measures

Record launch version/date, page views, purchases, non-creator uses, return
uses, refunds, and useful feedback from Creator Studio when accessible.
No views suggests a discovery issue; views without purchases suggests a
listing/value/price issue; purchases without useful runs suggests onboarding
or product friction. Collect evidence before choosing a price or scope change.
